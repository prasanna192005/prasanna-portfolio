# Design System: Artisan-Tech Archive (v2.7)

## Core Philosophy
The "Artisan-Tech" aesthetic is a high-fidelity fusion of traditional Indian heritage (Sanskriti) and modern digital engineering (Vigyanam). It prioritizes structural hierarchy, tactile textures, and meaningful motion.

## Color Palette
- **Royal Indigo (#2563EB)**: Primary brand color. Used for high-impact backgrounds and primary interactive states.
- **Martyr Red (#FF1F00)**: Secondary accent. Used for critical action nodes, system alerts, and heritage motifs.
- **Temple Gold (#FFA502)**: Tertiary accent. Used for decorative borders and subtle highlights.
- **Patrika Cream (#FDFBF7)**: Primary surface. A warm, tactile off-white that mimics traditional khadi paper.
- **Abyssal Black (#121212)**: Primary text and structural borders.
- **Altar Crimson (#D62238)**: Heritage motif accent.

## Typography
1. **Heritage Narrative (Serif)**
   - Role: Emotional storytelling, high-fidelity headers.
   - Styling: Italicized, tight tracking, fluid and calligraphic.
   - Example: *"The grace of tradition meet the precision of tomorrow."*

2. **Structural Identity (Sans-Serif / Helvetica)**
   - Role: System navigation, primary labels, bold identity.
   - Styling: Bold, uppercase, tracked-in for impact.
   - Example: *"ARCHIVE_SYSTEM_ID_01"*

3. **Technical Metadata (Pixel / Monospace)**
   - Role: System status, technical markers, low-level nodes.
   - Styling: Small caps, wide tracking (0.3em+), monospaced.
   - Example: *"INITIALIZING_NEURAL_LINK... OK"*

## Visual Elements
- **Temple Brackets**: L-shaped decorative strokes on corners of containers.
- **Mandala Motifs**: Geometric SVG patterns used as watermarks or animated anchors.
- **Khadi Texture**: A subtle grain overlay applied to surfaces to create a tactile, paper-like feel.
- **Peacock Borders**: Layered, colorful borders at the top or bottom of sections.

## Motion & Behavior
- **Floating Rise**: Subtle upward translation on hover (Y: -10px to -20px).
- **Elastic Expansion**: Spring-based scaling on interaction (Scale: 1.05).
- **Centric Rotation**: SVG motifs rotating on their center point during transitions.
- **Scroll Sync**: Artifacts (SVGs/Images) entering the viewport with varying speeds and rotations.

## Design Rules for AI
1. **Layering**: Always use layered z-indices to allow decorative assets to overlap structural borders.
2. **Contrast**: High contrast between Patrika Cream surfaces and Abyssal Black text.
3. **Hierarchy**: Use Pixel fonts to ground the Serif/Sans-Serif typography in technical reality.
4. **Spacing**: Generous whitespace to allow high-fidelity assets to breathe.
